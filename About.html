British mezzo-soprano Phoebe Rayner is a recent graduate of the Royal College of Music and Royal Northern College of Music. She is a Stoller Hall Emerging Artist and Britten Pears Young Artist alumni, specialising in contemporary music and the development of new works.

Recent operatic highlights include premiering the role of Monostatos in Damon Albarn’s The Magic Flute II: Le Malédiction (Le Lido, Paris), Flora in La Traviata (Barefoot Opera), Alcandro (cover) in L’Olimpiade (Vache Baroque), Nerone in Agrippina (Hampstead Garden Opera), La chatte/L'écureuil in L’enfant et les sortilèges (RCM Opera Studio), Concepción in L'heure Espagnole (Ernest Reed Symphonic Orchestra), Stewardess in Flight (directed by Jeremy Sams, RCM Opera Studio), Hänsel in Hänsel und Gretel (Westminster Opera Company), Jacinta/Lieutenant II in Rip Van Winkle (Gothic Opera), and a Spring Opera Showcase for Clonter Opera (directed by Peter Relton).

On the concert platform, she has performed Miss Donnithorne's Maggot (The Stoller Hall, Manchester), Pribaoutki (Britten Pears Contemporary Ensemble, The Aldeburgh Festival), and a solo recital as part of the Manchester Song Festival. Her collaborative work with digital artist Charlotte Young has been exhibited at the Lethaby Gallery, Copeland Gallery and SET Woolwich.
