Praised for her “coppery” and “richly coloured” mezzo, Phoebe Rayner (she/her) is a graduate of the Royal College of Music and Royal Northern College of Music. She is a recent Stoller Hall Emerging Artist and Britten Pears Young Artist specialising in contemporary music and the development of new works.

Operatic highlights include premiering the role of Monostatos in Damon Albarn’s The Magic Flute II: Le Malédiction (Théâtre du Lido), Melissa in La Liberazione di Ruggiero (Buxton International Festival), Flora in La Traviata (Hurn Court Opera and Barefoot Opera), Alcandro (cover) in L’Olimpiade (Vache Baroque), Nerone in Agrippina (HGO), La chatte/L'écureuil in L’enfant et les sortilèges (RCM), Concepción in L'heure Espagnole (ERSO), Stewardess in Flight (RCM), and Hänsel in Hänsel und Gretel (Westminster Opera Company).

On the concert platform, she has performed Miss Donnithorne's Maggot (The Stoller Hall, Manchester), Pribaoutki (The Aldeburgh Festival), and a solo recital as part of the Manchester Song Festival. Her collaborative work with digital artist Charlotte Young has been exhibited at the Lethaby Gallery, Copeland Gallery and SET Woolwich, with development supported by Hawkwood College and the Francis W Reckitt Arts Trust.

Upcoming: Dido in Dido and Aeneas (Prangins Baroque) , Cherubino in Le Nozze di Figaro (Oxford Opera, Family Matinee).
